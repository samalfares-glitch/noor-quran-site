Follow your own journey through the Quran, with the tools you need close at hand.

Read

Browse the mushaf by surah, juz or page. Save bookmarks and pick up where you left off.

Understand

Listen

Choose a reciter and follow along verse by verse. Recitation requires an internet connection.

App help

Here when you need it.

Can I read offline?

Quran text, translations and commentaries are included in the app. Streaming recitation requires internet access.

Where are my bookmarks?

Your bookmarks and reading position are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data.

How do I see a translation?

Tap a verse in the reader and select Translation, or switch to the translation view.

Is the app available yet?

The app is being prepared for release. Availability will be announced when it is ready.

Your information

Privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026. This policy describes version 1.0 of Noor Quran for iPhone and iPad.

Information stored on your device

Bookmarks, reading position, preferences and reading statistics are stored on your device. They are not transmitted to the app developer by the app. Removing the app deletes its locally stored data.

Analytics

Usage analytics are not enabled in this version of the app.

Recitation audio

When you play recitation, the app streams audio from EveryAyah.com. As with ordinary internet requests, that service may receive information such as your device’s IP address to deliver the audio.

Apple services

Apple may provide the developer with aggregated App Store statistics and crash reports from users who choose to share them. Apple’s own privacy practices apply to its services.

Changes and questions

If the app’s privacy practices change, this policy will be updated. A support contact will be provided on this page before the app is released.
